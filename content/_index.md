## Portuguese for true beginners

You don't need any Portuguese to start. Most of my Portuguese students know nothing, or only a few words, when we begin. We start with what you'll actually say, not with grammar charts.

**This is for you if:**

- You have a **Brazilian spouse or partner** and want to speak with them in their language
- You want to connect with **in-laws, family, and friends** in Brazil
- You're **traveling to Brazil** and want to order food, ask for directions, and get by
- You've tried apps and still **freeze** when someone speaks to you

**What we work on in the first classes:**

- Greetings, introductions, and small talk
- Ordering food, taking a taxi, shopping, and asking for help
- Everyday phrases you'll hear from family, like *tô com saudade* and *cê tá onde?*
- Pronunciation, especially the nasal sounds, with easy comparisons to English

{{< alert >}}
**No experience needed.** If "obrigado" is all you've got, that's the right place to start.
{{< /alert >}}

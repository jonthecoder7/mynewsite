## What I Do
 
I handle the planning, editing, and posting of your short-form content — Reels, TikToks, and everything in between. What I don't do is show up with a camera. You film the raw footage on your phone (I'll tell you exactly what to grab and how), and I turn it into a real content system from there: strategy, edits, captions, and a consistent posting schedule, without you having to think about any of it once the footage is in my hands.
 
This keeps costs down for you and turnaround fast for both of us — no travel, no scheduling a shoot day, no waiting on a crew.
 
## How It Works

## How the classes work

Before your first class, you answer a quick questionnaire about your goals, your level, and what you enjoy. I use your answers to prepare every class around you.

- **Private and online.** One-on-one on Google Meet.
- **You speak from the first class.** Every lesson is built around things you will actually say.
- **Spoken Portuguese, not just written.** You learn *tô*, *tá*, *cê* and *pra*, the way people really say them.
- **Pronunciation coaching.** Nasal sounds like *ão*, *bom* and *com*, and the soft *d* and *t* sounds, with easy comparisons to English.
- **Material after every class.** Notes, slides, flashcards, and short voice-note homework you can send to a real person.
- **Explained in English, practiced in Portuguese.**

### What a 1-hour class looks like

1. **Welcome:** we chat about your day, in Portuguese.
2. **Conversation:** you speak, I listen and help with new words.
3. **Main topic:** we learn something useful, like travel, family, or everyday life.
4. **Practice:** we use the new vocabulary in role-plays and real situations.
5. **Wrap-up:** I give you feedback and your homework.
